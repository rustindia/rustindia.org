Rust India is the community for Rust programmers and enthusiasts across the country. Whether you have been writing Rust for years or you are just curious about what the language is about, there is a place for you here.

The community lives on a few channels: Discord for day-to-day chat, Matrix for decentralized discussion, and X and Bluesky for announcements. Meetups and workshops are announced on the [home page](/) and the [archive schedule](/archive/schedule) as they are planned.

The best way to get involved is to [join the community](/community) — pick the channels that suit you, say hello, and ask questions. Nobody here will judge you for asking.

This site is run by community members, and the blog is open to anyone who wants to write. See [write for the blog](/blog/write-for-the-blog/) to learn how to contribute a post.
